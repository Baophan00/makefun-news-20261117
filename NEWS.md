# News shortlist — 17 November 2026

Eight consequential world stories reported on 17 November 2026.

## 1. Trump's Asia trip and a planned three-way summit
Trump left Washington for Asia and said he may meet Kim Jong Un. He arrived in Manila for ASEAN and Quad talks. The first Putin-Trump-Xi trilateral was set for Shenzhen on 18-19 November.

## 2. Gulf escalation involving Iran and the US
Iran fired drones at a US radar at Bahrain's Sheikh Isa base. Mines hit five ships in Hormuz. The US struck an Iranian missile site on Qeshm island.

## 3. Ukraine war and settlement talks
Russia reported a record number of Ukrainian drones downed. The IMF said Ukraine could get about 1.6 billion dollars more. Europe joined settlement talks.

## 4. Gaza aid and West Bank measure
Germany urged Israel to allow more Gaza aid before winter. Israel declared a West Bank village a closed military zone.

## 5. Telegram blocked in Jordan and Iraq
Jordan and Iraq blocked Telegram.

## 6. WHO warning on drug-resistant infections
WHO warned drug-resistant infections could directly cause over 39 million deaths by 2050.

## 7. AI safety and financial-stability warnings
OpenAI unveiled a CSAM image classifier. The ECB and Fed warned AI could threaten financial stability.

## 8. Software outages in the US
US banking apps and United Airlines had software outages.
